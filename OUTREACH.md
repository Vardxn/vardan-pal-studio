# Outreach plan — Chanakyapuri, MKT, Chanakya

Goal: 10 premium businesses contacted per week, 1 paid job in 30 days.

## 1. Build the list (Google Maps, 1 hour)
Search: "restaurants Chanakyapuri", "cafes Khan Market", "lounges Chanakya Puri", "spas near Chanakyapuri", "salons Khan Market", "interior designers South Delhi".
Keep a sheet with: name, area, rating, website, phone, Instagram, what is wrong with their site.
Pick places that: have 4.2+ stars, have a weak site (or only Instagram / PDF menu), and look like they care about brand.

## 2. Check each site on your phone (5 min each)
Write down three facts, nothing vague:
- loads slowly or not (open it on mobile data)
- no WhatsApp or reserve button
- menu is a PDF / broken on phone

## 3. Walk-in pitch (best channel)
Go 3–5 pm (quiet hour). Ask for the owner or manager. Say:
"I'm Vardan, a web developer in Delhi. I looked at your site on my phone and made a one-page version for you. Can I show you in 30 seconds?"
Show it on your phone. Leave a card with the link. Don't discuss price unless asked.

## 4. WhatsApp / email (when you can't visit)
WhatsApp, one message, no attachment:
"Hi [Name], I'm Vardan, a web developer in New Delhi. I checked [place]'s website on my phone: [one real fact]. I made a quick mockup of how it could look: [link]. No obligation. If you'd like to see more, reply here."

Email subject: "[Place] website on mobile — quick mockup"
Body: 3 lines — the real fact, the mockup link, one question ("Who looks after your website?"). Send from a domain address once set up, not Gmail.

## 5. Follow-up
Day 3: "Did you get a chance to look? Happy to walk you through in 5 minutes."
Day 10: one last message, then stop. Never more than 3 contacts.

## 6. Rules
- Never claim past clients, rankings or lead numbers you don't have.
- Say "mockup" or "concept". Get the owner's OK before you publish anything with their name.
- Cold calls: skip numbers on the DND registry; keep calls short; ask if it's a good time.
- Price: Signature (from ₹34,900) first for premium places; mention Business as the smaller option.

## 7. First proof
Offer the first 1–2 businesses a lower price for a written testimonial and permission to show the site. Then add them to the homepage as real case studies.

## 8. Weekly tally
Contacted / replied / meeting / quote sent / paid. Review every Sunday and fix the weakest step.
