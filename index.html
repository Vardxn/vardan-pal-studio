Questionspage 7

Before you ask.

What does the Launch package include?

Launch includes a one-page premium website, responsive development, WhatsApp and call buttons, Google Maps, basic SEO setup, deployment, one revision round, and 15 days of post-launch support.

What is the difference between Business and Signature?

Business is designed for established businesses that need several pages, enquiry flows, trust sections, and local SEO. Signature adds a larger bespoke structure, richer motion, advanced intake flows, analytics, and a longer support period.

Do you build booking systems?

Standard packages can include appointment or enquiry request flows. Real-time calendars, dashboards, portals, and automation are scoped separately under Custom Systems.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If the scope grows, the website can be upgraded to a higher package. Confirm the upgrade and any price adjustment before additional work begins.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

Post-launch support is included for the period shown in each package. Ongoing maintenance and content updates can be quoted separately.

How do you make sure enquiries are relevant?

Instead of a bare contact form, enquiry flows ask for the service, budget and timeline before the message reaches your WhatsApp or inbox, so the first conversation starts with the facts.

What if I am not tech-savvy and don't want to manage software?

You don't have to touch any software. Deployment, your domain and your WhatsApp links are set up for you, so you can focus on running your business.
