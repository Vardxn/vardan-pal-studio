## 3. Service Tracks & Category Alignment

The studio operates across three distinct service tracks:

### A. Business Websites
- **Target Industries:** Salons, clinics, restaurants, consultants, local service businesses, real estate professionals, interior designers, photographers, and creative studios.
- **Standard Deliverables:** Premium responsive layout, mobile-first design, clear service & pricing rate card presentation, contact & enquiry paths, WhatsApp integration, technical SEO, cloud deployment, and SSL setup.
- **Applicable Tiers:** Launch, Business, Signature.

### B. Booking & Enquiry Experiences
- **Target Industries:** Clinics, salons, restaurants, wedding creators, consultants, and appointment-based practices.
- **Standard Deliverables:** Structured enquiry forms, appointment request flows, date/service selection controls, pre-filled WhatsApp handoffs, client-side input validation, and instant confirmation toasts.
- **Governance Rule:** Phrased strictly as "appointment request", "enquiry flow", or "booking request". Never promise real-time live calendar synchronization if the implementation relies on WhatsApp messaging.

### C. Custom Digital Systems
- **Target Businesses:** Organizations with repetitive manual workflows, teams needing customer portals, practices requiring lead pre-qualification, and automated CRM pipelines.
- **Standard Deliverables:** Custom dashboards, client portals, automated WhatsApp notifications, webhooks, and third-party database bridges.
- **Governance Rule:** Never include Custom Systems features inside the Launch or Business standard website packages.

## 4. Promise Alignment & Communication Governance

To maintain client trust and professional integrity, all studio communication adheres to strict promise boundaries:

### Approved Technical Promises
- Mobile-first development and responsive testing across 7 viewports
- Direct founder communication with software engineering expertise (NIT Srinagar)
- WhatsApp enquiry integration with pre-filled encoded messaging
- Core Web Vitals optimization and clean vanilla code (sub-second load times)
- Transparent scope and pricing with upfront rate cards
- Cloud deployment and SSL certificate configuration
- Technical SEO setup and Google Search Console indexing
- Structured enquiry flows that clarify budget, service, and timeline
- Post-launch support strictly according to the purchased package tier

### Prohibited & Exaggerated Claims
- ❌ **No guaranteed Google rankings** (e.g., "#1 on Google" replaced with "Technical SEO setup & Search Console configuration").
- ❌ **No guaranteed lead volume or sales** (replaced with "Clear enquiry paths & faster customer journeys").
- ❌ **No unlimited revisions or unlimited support** (each tier defines explicit revision counts and support days).
- ❌ **No full CMS in standard website tiers** (CMS implementation quoted separately).
- ❌ **No real-time booking engines** unless an actual calendar/booking engine is commissioned under Custom Systems.
- ❌ **No automated WhatsApp CRM** bundled inside standard website packages.
- ❌ **No ongoing maintenance** unless explicitly purchased under a retainer agreement.
- ❌ **No 24/7 support or instant turnaround promises.**
