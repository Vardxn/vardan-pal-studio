HERITAGE MEETS REFINEMENT

Reimagining Gastronomy Without Compromising Soul

At Malcha Table, every dish honors ancient regional culinary techniques—from 24-hour slow charcoal simmering in copper degs to hand-pounded mountain spice pastes from Kashmir and Kerala.

Framed by lush foliage in the diplomatic enclave of Chanakyapuri, our dining room provides an intimate, civilized respite from Delhi’s bustle for diplomatic delegates, visiting heads of state, and local connoisseurs.

"Subtle smoke, balanced spice, and ingredients sourced with meticulous integrity."
